# Dream Pixel privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Dream Pixel is a dream journal for Android, made by an independent developer. Contact: dreampixeljournal@gmail.com.

## Your journal stays on your phone
Everything you put in the journal stays on your phone: your nights, your words, how they felt, your recordings and what they say, your dream signs, your settings and your app lock. Dream Pixel has no account and no server for your journal. Nobody but you can read it.

## Speech recognition
Recordings are turned into words on your phone. The first time you record, the app downloads a speech model (about 190 MB) from huggingface.co. Like any download, this lets Hugging Face see your IP address. Nothing else about you is sent.

## What you choose to send
Something leaves your phone only when you press a button to send it:
- **"Tell the developer you'd pick …"**: the plan you would pick, its price and the date.
- **A letter from "Write to the developer"**: your words and the topic; the email address you typed, if you asked for a reply; your phone model, Android version and app version, if "Add phone model and app version" is ticked; the plan you picked in early access, if any; and the date.

It goes over an encrypted connection to a Google Sheet and an email in the developer's Google account, through Google Apps Script. It is used only to read it and, if you asked, to answer you. It is never sold or shared. Until it is sent, it waits on your phone. To have it deleted, write to dreampixeljournal@gmail.com.

## Backups
If Android backup is on, Android copies your journal (not your recordings) to your Google account, encrypted with your screen lock when you have one. When you move to a new phone by cable or Wi-Fi, your recordings move with it. The app lock code is never copied: you set it up again on a new phone. You control backups in Android's settings.

## Ratings
Once, after you are given the whole journal in early access, Dream Pixel asks Google Play to show its rating window. Google decides whether to show it. A rating you leave goes to Google Play, under Google's terms.

## Permissions
- **Microphone**: only while you record.
- **Notifications and alarms**: only the reminders and the wake-back-to-bed alarm you switch on.
- **Internet**: the speech model download and what you choose to send.
- **Storage** (Android 9 and older): to save an export to Downloads.

## No ads, no analytics
Dream Pixel shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking of any kind.

## Children
Dream Pixel is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling Dream Pixel deletes your journal from your phone. Copies in your Google backup are managed in Android's and Google's settings. For anything you sent, write to dreampixeljournal@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
